I keep my iPhone display in grayscale—it makes everything dull and less alluring[^1].

The problem is that sometimes I really do want color, like when I'm viewing or editing photos, or looking at driving directions.

This is the simple setup I use to temporarily enable color.

**A shortcut to toggle grayscale on and off.** On iPhone, go to Settings → Accessibility → Accessibility Shortcut. Enable **Color Filters**. Now triple-click your side button to toggle grayscale on and off.

**An automation to re-enable grayscale every morning.** Sometimes I forget to toggle grayscale back on. So I make this automatic using a Shortcut.

1. Open the Shortcuts app and create a new shortcut.
2. If you have the new (as of 2026) Siri with enhanced AI, you can just ask: "Turn on color filters at 5 am every day". Otherwise, install [this shortcut](https://www.icloud.com/shortcuts/46f1d341a34e4670a29a76252fa82e48).

[^1]: [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbr.2023.100294](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbr.2023.100294)
